Common questions

Which consultation is right for me?

New to this work, or just want to understand your chart? Start with Learn Your Natal Chart. For a partnership — romantic or otherwise — the Relationship Reading compares two charts side by side. If you're working with patterns in identity, work, or the inner life — Personal Growth. If you're working with physical patterns like fatigue, digestion, sleep, autoimmune symptoms, or hormonal cycles — Physical Health. If you suspect Growth and Health are connected — the Bundle gives you both at a saving.

I don't have a birth chart yet. Can I still book?

Yes. The booking link takes you to the system that will guide you through creating your chart — birth date, birth time, and birth location are required. Once your chart is on file, you complete your intake form and book your session.

How does the Relationship Reading work with two people?

Each person creates their own birth chart with their own email and consent. At checkout you look up your chart, then the other person's by their email, so both are on the order before the call. No intake form is needed — the two charts are the material we work from.

What if I need to pause the intake form?

The intake form is saved by your email. You can pause and resume at any time. We recommend completing it at least 48 hours before your session so Lisa has time to review it alongside your chart.

How does the 3 Physical Health bundle work?

You complete one intake form before your first consultation. After that, your daily entries in the Health Journal are what prepare the second and third sessions — how your constitution is actually responding between visits tells us more than filling the same form in again. Daily journaling is expected, because the follow-up sessions are built on it.

Can I reschedule?

Yes — reschedule through the booking confirmation email. For bundles, sessions need to maintain the minimum spacing (7 days for the Growth + Health bundle, 14 days for the 3 Physical Health bundle).

Is this covered by insurance or FSA?

No. Astrological consultations are not covered by insurance, and they are not FSA-eligible expenses. Payment is made directly at booking.

What if I'm not sure I'm ready for the deep work?

The Guided Onboarding Intake Call at $100 is the right starting place. We walk through filling out your intake form together — no prep needed, just bring yourself. You decide afterward whether to book a full consultation.
