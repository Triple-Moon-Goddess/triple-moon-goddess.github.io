Why ninety and not thirty

Thirty minutes can describe you. Ninety is long enough to descend — and to come back up holding something you can use on Monday.

A short reading has to stay on the surface. It names the placement, offers the keyword, and time's up. That's a fine introduction, and it's genuinely all some people want.

This is the other thing. Ninety minutes is enough room to find where a pattern started, stay with it past the point where it gets uncomfortable, and then build the way out — the herbs, the diet, the practice, the sentence you'll repeat to yourself for a year. This is the work I was made for, and after fifteen years it's the only kind I want to be doing.

It isn't for everyone. It's for the person who wants to do their own healing work and would rather have the real answer than a comfortable one. If that's not you, no hard feelings — the free apps and the small readings are genuinely yours to keep.

How I sell: I tell you once what's available, and then I leave you alone. No follow-up sequence, no countdown timer, no manufactured scarcity. If it's your work, you'll know — and the door stays open whenever you get there.
