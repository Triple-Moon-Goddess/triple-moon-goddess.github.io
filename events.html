How to Use Your Natal Chart for Personal Growth and Health Improvement

Your natal chart is more than a personality profile. It's a constitutional map — one that points to how your body processes stress, what it needs to thrive, and where your personal growth edges live.

In this session, Lisa Hagan of Triple Moon Goddess walks you through the framework behind integrative astrology: how your chart reveals both your physical constitution and your personal growth patterns — and how those two layers inform each other.

You'll see a live demonstration of the TMG platform — birth data in, constitutional and growth profile out — and leave with a new way of reading the chart you were born with.
