About this application

Triple Moon Goddess

Triple Moon Goddess is a web application for integrative astrology. You give it your birth data; it maps your constitution — the pattern underneath your physical health and your personal growth — and returns practical profiles you can act on: diet, herbs, timing, and reflection prompts drawn from that pattern. Everything it produces is a pattern to explore, never a diagnosis.

For individuals

Charts and profiles, no account

Enter your birth data and get your natal chart, constitutional profile, and growth patterns. The free apps — chart, health journal, health blueprint, Tea Moon, daily widget — need no account, no password, and no Google sign-in.

For practitioners

The same engine inside a practice

Subscribing practitioners see chart-derived outputs for the people they work with — placements, constitutional profile, pattern scores — in the vocabulary of their own field. Practitioner, partner, and administrator accounts sign in with Google.

For booking

90-minute sessions with Lisa

Booking a session checks availability on Lisa’s own business calendar through the Google Calendar API and creates the appointment, with your email added to the event so you receive the invitation and the video link.

What the application does with your data.

Birth data and journal entries are held apart from your identity: your email lives in an encrypted vault of its own, and the rest is connected by identifiers rather than by name. Google user data is limited to sign-in and that one calendar — never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train any AI model. The detail is in why this app asks for Google access and in the privacy policy.

Why ninety and not thirty

Thirty minutes can describe you. Ninety is long enough to descend — and to come back up holding something you can use on Monday.

A short reading has to stay on the surface. It names the placement, offers the keyword, and time's up. That's a fine introduction, and it's genuinely all some people want.

This is the other thing. Ninety minutes is enough room to find where a pattern started, stay with it past the point where it gets uncomfortable, and then build the way out — the herbs, the diet, the practice, the sentence you'll repeat to yourself for a year. This is the work I was made for, and after fifteen years it's the only kind I want to be doing.

It isn't for everyone. It's for the person who wants to do their own healing work and would rather have the real answer than a comfortable one. If that's not you, no hard feelings — the free apps and the small readings are genuinely yours to keep.

How I sell: I tell you once what's available, and then I leave you alone. No follow-up sequence, no countdown timer, no manufactured scarcity. If it's your work, you'll know — and the door stays open whenever you get there.

Transparency

Why this app asks for Google access.

Two permissions, and no others.

Sign in with Google — email and basic profile
Granted by practitioners, partners, and administrators. We receive your email address and Google account ID, and use them to authenticate your account and match it to your subscription. Seekers never sign in with Google at all.
Google Calendar
Granted once by Lisa, for her own business calendar. Used to check availability and create your booking, with your email added to the event so you get the invitation and the Meet link. No health, journal, or birth data ever reaches a calendar event.

We do not request or receive your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Photos, or your own calendar. Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train any AI model. Full detail is in Section VI of the privacy policy; access can be revoked anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Triple Moon Goddess's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
