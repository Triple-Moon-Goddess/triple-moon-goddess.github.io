Your Data: What We Store and How It's Protected

Triple Moon Goddess is built so your identity and your data are kept apart. No single record holds both.

What we store, and where

  • Your email is stored once, on its own, in an encrypted vault. It is looked up only through a one-way code, never searched as plain text, and is never copied into your other records.
  • Your birth data (date, time, and place) is stored once, on its own, in a separate encrypted vault, used only to generate your chart, transits, and readings.
  • We do not store your name. The only time a name is held is if you sign a legal waiver — and then only as an encrypted signature tied to that consent.
  • Everything else in the app is linked together only by an anonymous access code — never by your name, email, or birth data.

Encryption

Your email and birth data are encrypted at rest (Google Cloud KMS) and in transit (HTTPS). We never sell your data.

Your birth data stays in the vault

Your birth data never leaves its encrypted vault to be shown in the apps — not to other users, and not to practitioners. The calculations that use it run on our secure servers, not in your browser. The one exception: Lisa, the site owner, can view your birth data through an internal admin tool solely to correct a data-entry error, such as a mistyped birth date. You remain the owner of your data.

Research

Your birth data is never used for research. Any anonymous signals we keep for research contain no name, no email, and no birth data.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time. After you delete, anything retained for research is anonymous — no name, email, or birth data remains, so nothing links back to you.

Built-in enforcement

These rules are enforced automatically in our software: an internal privacy check blocks any change that would store a client's name, search an email in plain text, or move birth data outside its vault.

IXSecurity Measures

Encryption of Sensitive Data (Managed-Key Cloud KMS)

Your most sensitive data — your email, your birth data, and, for practitioner accounts, client clinical notes and psychological readings — is encrypted at rest with a dedicated Google Cloud Key Management Service (KMS) key. The key lives server-side only. Encryption and decryption happen inside our Cloud Functions; the key is never sent to, or held by, any browser.

Your email and your birth data are each stored once, in their own access-controlled vault. Email is encrypted in one vault and located only through a one-way lookup code (a SHA-256 hash of your email) — it is never stored or searched as plain text. Birth data is encrypted in a separate vault, keyed only by your anonymous access code. Neither vault is readable by the apps or by other users; only our server functions can decrypt, and only for a permitted purpose such as sending your chart by email.

Practitioner-entered client data — client email, clinical notes, and psychological readings — is encrypted with the same managed key on the server at the moment it is saved, and the plaintext is removed. Even if the database were fully compromised, these records would be unreadable, because the key is not in the database and never leaves the server. A practitioner can decrypt only the specific clients assigned to them: every decryption is gated server-side by the practitioner's authenticated account plus proof they own that client assignment (or by an authorised admin).

The earlier passphrase-based, in-browser encryption scheme has been retired. All sensitive data written today uses the managed-key model above. A small amount of data sealed under the old passphrase scheme before migration is not recoverable and is clearly marked as such where it appears.

The following data is protected by Firebase Security Rules rather than field-level encryption: natal chart outputs, client intake answers, intake match results, journal entries, and Soul module session data.

Infrastructure Security

All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Data is encrypted at rest by default (Firebase/Firestore), with the sensitive fields above additionally encrypted under our own Cloud KMS key. Your birth data is write-only from the browser — it can be sent in to be stored, but no app or user can read raw birth data back; all chart, transit, and reading calculations run on our secure servers. API keys are stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager — never in code. Firebase Security Rules restrict database access to each authenticated user's own data. The admin portal is restricted to a single authorised account, and admin access to raw birth data (used only to correct a data-entry error) is audit-logged. Identity data and health data are held in separate collections — no single record contains both.

No security system is 100% impenetrable. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact Lisa@TripleMoonGoddess.com immediately.

XIIIPractitioner App Data

The Integrative Practitioner, Soul Pattern, and Constitutional Health apps are separate paid-tier applications. Their data architecture differs from the individual seeker apps.

What Practitioners Enter

Practitioners enter client birth data (date, time, location) to generate a natal chart and derived outputs. The practitioner also enters a client email address as the client identifier within their account. That email is encrypted on our server with our managed Cloud KMS key at the moment it is saved — the plaintext is removed and never stored unencrypted anywhere. Even if the database were compromised, client email records would be unreadable, because the key is held server-side and never leaves it.

What Practitioners See

The practitioner view surfaces chart-derived outputs: sign placements, house positions, constitutional profile, pattern group scores, and comparison signals. Practitioners do not see raw birth data displayed back to them after chart generation — the chart describes the person through derived outputs, it does not display the underlying date, time, and location as identifying fields.

Client Birth Data

Birth data entered by a practitioner is used solely to calculate the natal chart for that client record. It is encrypted with our managed Cloud KMS key and held in a dedicated, access-controlled vault keyed by the client's anonymous access code — never exposed to the apps and never readable back as raw birth data. A practitioner can reach only the clients assigned to them, enforced server-side. It is not shared with other practitioners, not used for advertising, and not linked to any identity record in the research corpus.

Research Data from Practitioner Sessions

Soul module sessions, pattern scores, comparison signals, and practitioner follow-up feedback may contribute to the TMG longitudinal research corpus. All research records are pseudonymized before inclusion. The pseudonymized record contains pattern-level and astrological signal data only — for example, whether a particular planetary aspect correlates with a reported pattern across a population. No name, email, birth date, birth time, or birth location is included in any published or shared research output. The link between a client record and its pseudonymized research entry is stored separately from the research corpus and is never published.

Encryption

Practitioner client data — client email, clinical notes, and psychological readings — is encrypted at rest with our managed Google Cloud KMS key. Encryption and decryption happen server-side; the key is never held in the practitioner's browser. Access is gated by the practitioner's authenticated account plus ownership of the specific client assignment. See Section IX for full technical detail. (The earlier passphrase-based scheme has been retired; a small amount of data sealed under it before migration is not recoverable.)
