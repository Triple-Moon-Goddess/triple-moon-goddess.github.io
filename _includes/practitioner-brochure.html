HIPAA & your practice

The short answer

Triple Moon Goddess signs a Business Associate Agreement with your practice as part of the subscription process. Your clients decide what you see, and clinical judgment stays with you.

How a client record works
  1. Your client creates their own account and enters their own birth data.
  2. Birth data is held in a separate, pseudonymized table and stays there. The assessment uses the results.
  3. Your client completes the intake themselves.
  4. You look up your client by email. Once they approve, their record is available only to you.
  5. Optional client codes: work under a code and keep identifying details in your own handwritten log.
