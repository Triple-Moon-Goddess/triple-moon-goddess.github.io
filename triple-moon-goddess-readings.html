Common questions

Which reading should I choose?

Each reading focuses on one area of life. Choose Romantic for love and partnership, Career for work and vocation, Family for emotional inheritance and dynamics, or Soul Purpose for your karmic path. The Archetype Reading covers your dominant patterns across the whole chart.

How is the session credit applied?

Every reading carries credit equal to its price toward a 90-minute consultation with Lisa. The credit applies automatically when you book — no code needed.

Do I need a birth chart already?

No. The checkout link guides you through entering your birth date, time, and location. Your reading is generated from that exact data.

How is the reading delivered?

Each reading is generated as a PDF using the Integrative Pattern Astrology framework, delivered to you so you can keep and revisit it.

What's the difference between a reading and a consultation?

A reading is a focused PDF on one part of your chart. A consultation is a live 90-minute session with Lisa for the deeper work. Readings are a starting point — and the credit carries forward when you're ready for a session.
