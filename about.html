About Lisa

For years I managed anxiety, PTSD, MCAS, Hashimoto's, and severe post-menopausal symptoms with partial answers at best.

Herbs didn't work for my body. Diets helped some things but never the whole picture. Standard approaches — conventional and alternative — kept giving me pieces.

Nobody was going to hand me the answer.
So I built it myself.

Triple Moon Goddess is the synthesis of 15 years of astrological practice. I attended the Academy of AstroPsychology, the School of Evolutionary Herbalism, and studied Traditional Chinese Medicine — all applied personally to understand a complex, multi-system constitution.

I take Western medication and HRT. TMG fills the gaps modern medicine can't fill. It works alongside, not instead of.
