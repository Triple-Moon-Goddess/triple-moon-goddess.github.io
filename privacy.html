Your Data: What We Store and How It's Protected

Triple Moon Goddess is built so your identity and your data are kept apart. No single record holds both.

What we store, and where

  • Your email is stored once in our database, on its own, in an encrypted vault. It is looked up only through a keyed one-way fingerprint, never searched as plain text, and is never copied into your other records in our database. The services that send your receipts, invitations and newsletters also receive it (see Section V).
  • Your birth data (date, time, and place) is stored once, on its own, in a separate encrypted vault, used only to generate your chart, transits, and readings.
  • We do not store your name. The only time a name is held is if you sign a legal waiver — and then only as an encrypted signature tied to that consent.
  • Everything else in the app is linked together only by an anonymous access code — never by your name, email, or birth data.

Encryption

Your email and birth data are encrypted at rest (Google Cloud KMS) and in transit (HTTPS). We never sell your data.

Your birth data stays in the vault

Your birth data never leaves its encrypted vault to be shown in the apps — not to other users, and not to practitioners. The calculations that use it run on our secure servers, not in your browser. The one exception: Lisa, the site owner, can view your birth data through an internal admin tool solely to correct a data-entry error, such as a mistyped birth date. You remain the owner of your data.

Research

Your birth data is never used for research. The research signals we keep — anonymous counts of how strongly each chart placement resonated — contain no name, no email, no birth data and no access code.

Deleting your data

You can delete your data at any time. After you delete, the only things kept are the anonymous research counts above and payment records reduced to amounts and status, so nothing links back to you. Backups age out within 98 days (Section VIII).

Built-in enforcement

These rules are enforced automatically in our software: an internal privacy check blocks any change that would store a client's name, search an email in plain text, or move birth data outside its vault.

IXSecurity Measures

Encryption of Sensitive Data (Managed-Key Cloud KMS)

Your most sensitive data — your email, your birth data, and, for practitioner accounts, practitioner notes and psychological readings — is encrypted at rest with a dedicated Google Cloud Key Management Service (KMS) key. The key lives server-side only. Encryption and decryption happen inside our Cloud Functions; the key is never sent to, or held by, any browser.

Your email and your birth data are each stored once, in their own access-controlled vault. Email is encrypted in one vault and located only through a keyed one-way fingerprint (HMAC-SHA256 with a secret key held in Google Secret Manager) — it is never stored or searched as plain text in our database. Birth data is encrypted in a separate vault, keyed only by your anonymous access code. Neither vault is readable by the apps or by other users; only our server functions can decrypt, and only for a permitted purpose such as sending your chart by email.

Practitioner records about a client — notes and psychological readings — are encrypted with the same managed key on the server at the moment they are saved, and the plaintext is removed. Even if the database were fully compromised, these records would be unreadable, because the key is not in the database and never leaves the server. A practitioner can decrypt only the records of clients who accepted that practitioner's invitation: every decryption is checked on the server against the practitioner's signed-in account and the client's consent (or is made by the administrator).

The following data is protected by Firebase Security Rules rather than field-level encryption: natal chart outputs, client intake answers, intake match results, journal entries, and Soul module session data.

Infrastructure Security

All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+). Data is encrypted at rest by default (Firebase/Firestore), with the sensitive fields above additionally encrypted under our own Cloud KMS key. Your birth data is write-only from the browser — it can be sent in to be stored, but no app or user can read raw birth data back; all chart, transit, and reading calculations run on our secure servers. API keys are stored in Google Cloud Secret Manager — never in code. Firebase Security Rules restrict database access to each authenticated user's own data, and a practitioner's access to a client's records to clients who gave consent. Practitioners and the administrator must use two-step sign-in and must have signed in within the last hour; signed-in sessions end after 30 minutes without activity. The admin portal is restricted to a single authorised account, and admin access to raw birth data (used only to correct a data-entry error) is audit-logged. Identity data and health data are held in separate collections — no single record contains both.

No security system is 100% impenetrable. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact Lisa@TripleMoonGoddess.com immediately.

Data Breach Notification

If a personal data breach occurs, we will act without undue delay to investigate and contain it. Where your personal information is affected, we will notify you, and any regulator, as required by applicable state breach-notification laws, including California Civil Code § 1798.82, without unreasonable delay. Because your identity data and your health data are held in physically separate Firestore collections, a breach affecting one does not necessarily expose the other.

A practitioner invites a client by entering the client's email address. If that email already has a chart with us, the client receives an invitation that works once and expires after 7 days. Practitioners may write notes, psychological readings, results, and follow-ups about a client who has shared their records.
