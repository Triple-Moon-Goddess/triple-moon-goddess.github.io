IAbout This Policy

This policy covers consumer health data as defined by Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373) and similar state laws, including Nevada's and Connecticut's. It applies to the Triple Moon Goddess apps and website, operated by Lisa Hagan, 3654 Thornton Ave, Unit #748, Fremont, CA 94536, United States. Our general Privacy Policy covers everything else.

We collect consumer health data only with your consent, or when it is necessary to provide a service you asked for. We do not collect precise location, and we do not use geofencing.

IIIWhere It Comes From

  • You, when you use the apps, answer questions, or speak with Lisa in a session.
  • A practitioner you have chosen to share your records with, who writes notes about you.
  • Our software, which derives your constitutional profile from the birth data you enter.

VYour Rights and How to Use Them

  • Know and access: ask whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, and get a copy of it, with a list of every third party it was shared with and how to contact each one.
  • Delete: delete your data yourself in the app (Health Journal: Settings → Delete All Data; chart page: Delete Data), or ask us. We delete it from our live systems at once and tell our processors to delete it too; deleted files clear from storage within 14 days, and it ages out of our encrypted backups within 98 days.
  • Withdraw consent: stop future collection by deleting your data or not using the feature, turn off journal sharing, or stop sharing with your practitioner altogether, in the journal's Settings → Practitioner (or email us). Stopping sharing deletes nothing. Your practitioner can no longer see your records; the notes they wrote about you are their own records, which they keep and can still read, edit or delete. You control your records and your practitioner controls their notes, as with any professional's notes. Their notes are deleted when their account closes.

To make a request, email Lisa@TripleMoonGoddess.com. We answer within 45 days (one 45-day extension if needed, and we will tell you why). Requests are free, up to twice a year.

Appeals

If we refuse a request, reply to our answer with the word "appeal". Lisa will review it and answer in writing within 45 days, explaining the decision. If the appeal is refused, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General (atg.wa.gov/file-complaint) or your own state's attorney general.
